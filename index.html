Questions

What families ask us most

Are overseas Pakistanis eligible?

Yes. Registrations are open for Pakistani residents and overseas clients.

Are flights included?

Package prices exclude air tickets. We book group fares for you and confirm them separately.

How much is the deposit?

A non-refundable deposit secures your booking. We confirm the exact amount and balance schedule for your package when you book.

Is Qurbani included?

Yes, Qurbani is included in every package.

Which documents do I need?

A machine-readable passport valid until 15 December 2027, CNIC or NICOP, your full address and contact details, next of kin’s CNIC and contact details, 4 photos (4x3 cm) and your blood group.
